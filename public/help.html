Facilitator run sheet

Live survey game for a GAHAR records and health information session. You share the big screen (/host); each participant plays on their own phone or laptop.

Before the session

  • Open /host in a browser tab, enter the PIN, press Create game. Test it once on your own phone.
  • Share the browser tab (not the whole desktop) in Zoom or Teams. Turn on "share computer sound" only if you want the beeps.
  • Keep the lobby on screen: the join address and the 4-digit room code are large. Post the address and code in the chat as well.
  • Say the rules aloud: look at the shared screen, answer on your own device, faster right answers score more.
  • Press H to hide the control bar before sharing if you do not want it seen.

During each round

  1. Scene: lines appear one by one. Read the key lines aloud. Press Space once to show everything at once, again for the question.
  2. Question: the timer runs and "N / total answered" fills up. It closes by itself when time ends or everyone has answered. Use + to add 15 seconds, R to close early.
  3. Reveal: bars show how the room answered. The best option is highlighted; Knock-out options carry a stamp. Discuss the "why" and the real case, then press L or Space.
  4. Leaderboard: top 5. Space starts the next round. After the last round you get the podium.

Keyboard

Space or →Next step (skips the scene animation first)
RReveal now (close the question)
+Add 15 seconds to the open question
LLeaderboard (from the reveal)
HHide or show the control bar
QShow or hide the invite: QR code, link with the room code, copy buttons for chat
MSound on or off

If something goes wrong

  • You refreshed or closed the host tab: reopen /host and log in again. The game continues exactly where it was.
  • A participant lost connection or refreshed: they reopen the address and rejoin silently with the same name and score. If they cleared their browser, they can join again with the code (late join is allowed).
  • Someone joined after round 1: they play from the current round; their report covers the rounds they were in.
  • The room is stuck waiting for one person: press R to reveal.
  • Double click: a second click on a step that already moved on is ignored.
  • Server restarted: state is saved. Reload the host page; players reconnect by themselves within a few seconds.

After the session

  • Tell participants to tap Open my report on their phone. It shows each answer against the best one, points, Knock-outs hit, the reasoning, the standards and the real case. They can print it or save it as PDF. Reports stay available for 14 days.
  • Use the podium for a short debrief: which Knock-out was chosen most, what surprised people.
  • Arabic text was drafted for review by a native speaker; say so if participants point out wording.
